# 🫀 Heart Disease Analysis Dashboard

An interactive, dark-themed clinical intelligence dashboard built in **Power BI** to analyze patient survival rates, critical biomarkers, and lifestyle risk factors associated with heart disease.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

In healthcare analytics, turning complex clinical records into clear, actionable insights is a matter of life and death. This project processes patient data to uncover crucial survival trends across age brackets, vital biomarkers, and compound chronic conditions.

---

## 🚀 Key Insights & Findings

* **Overall Survival Rate:** **68.04%** across the analyzed clinical dataset (132 survivors vs. 62 recorded fatalities)[cite: 1].
* **Age Cliff:** Survival rates drop sharply from **76.74%** in the 40–50 age group down to **23.08%** for patients in the 71+ bracket[cite: 1].
* **Biomarker Monitoring:** Tracks key clinical metrics including **Serum Creatinine** and **Ejection Fraction** across demographic segments[cite: 1].
* **Comorbidity Mapping:** Evaluates the compound impact of lifestyle risk factors such as **Smoking, High Blood Pressure, Anemia, and Diabetes**[cite: 1].

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack & Features

* **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI, DAX
* **Design Strategy:** Custom dark mode UI built for high visual contrast, executive readability, and modern aesthetics[cite: 1].
* **Interactivity:** Dynamic demographic filtering (Male / Female gender slicers)[cite: 1] and multi-card KPI tracking[cite: 1].
* **Data Visualizations:** Custom trend lines, distribution cards, and comparative bar matrices.
